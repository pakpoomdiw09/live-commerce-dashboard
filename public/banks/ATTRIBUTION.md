## Temporary fallback icons

BOC and CLICX have no installed bank logo. SnapBot shows its generic bank icon and a Thai label indicating that the actual logo is pending. The generic fallback is application UI, not a bank mark. The manifest records why an asset was not installed. Do not replace either fallback with an unlicensed download or another institution's logo.
